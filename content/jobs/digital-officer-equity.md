Promoted adoption of digital banking products by assessing customer needs and
onboarding an average of 20 business and retail customers per day onto mobile
and online banking platforms.

Provided frontline technical support for digital banking services,
troubleshooting account access and platform issues while explaining technical
concepts to non-technical users, improving product adoption and customer
satisfaction.

Collected and synthesized customer feedback on USSD banking services,
collaborating with internal product and operations teams to recommend workflow
improvements that enhanced the user experience for customers with limited
internet connectivity.
