Developed a centralized Single Sign-On (SSO) platform using the MERN stack,
JWT authentication, and REST APIs to provide secure, unified access across six
institutional research applications serving hundreds of researchers, staff, and patients.

Collaborated with researchers and end users to gather requirements, refine
authentication workflows, and implement improvements that reduced repeated login events by approximately 90% while maintaining secure access controls.

Designed and executed integration and functional test cases, identified and
resolved defects, and supported production deployment to help deliver a stable
and reliable authentication platform.
